Privacy policy

Drag Tea Served Publisher · last updated 8 October 2026

Drag Tea Served Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used by Matt (Drag Tea Served) to publish and schedule videos on his own social media accounts. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app accesses

  • Sign-in permissions (access tokens) for Matt's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts, granted by Matt through each platform's official sign-in.
  • Basic information about those accounts needed to post (account or channel names and IDs), the videos Matt chooses to publish, and their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and scheduled times.

How it's used

Only to upload, schedule and manage Matt's own posts, and to add details such as thumbnails, related videos and comments to them. The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or anyone else, does not use data for advertising, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Where it's stored

Access tokens and scheduled videos are stored privately on Matt's computer and in a private GitHub repository used to run the schedule. Videos are deleted from there once they're posted.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube Data API; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time from each platform's settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To have any stored data deleted, email the address below; it will be deleted within 30 days.
